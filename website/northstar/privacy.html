Northstar privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026 · Northstar by Blackhaven

Northstar is a personal workspace for records, tasks, notes, finances and attachments. This policy describes the native iPhone, iPad and Mac apps.

On your device

Your workspace, imported documents, saved tool results and local recovery copies are stored on your device. Guest workspaces are separate from signed-in workspaces and do not upload to Northstar’s iCloud sync or backup services. You choose which documents to import and which files to export.

Ask and on-device processing

In builds that include Ask, Northstar searches your imported documents and saved records, performs ledger calculations, and uses Apple’s on-device language model when available. Ask does not send your prompts or evidence to a cloud AI provider. Conversations, reviewed memories, evidence-review jobs and activity are saved as workspace data and are included when you choose workspace sync or backup. They are not sent to Blackhaven.

The search index is a disposable local copy of searchable evidence. It is excluded from device backup and can be rebuilt from workspace records. Private workspace files use complete file protection on iPhone and iPad; operations pause when protected data is unavailable. On Mac, Northstar uses its app sandbox and private file permissions. These are operating-system protections; Northstar does not claim separate application-level or end-to-end encryption for its workspace storage or iCloud features.

Memory corrections and deletion

You can correct or delete reviewed memories and delete conversations. Deleting a memory removes it from future retrieval and clears the local search cache. A synchronized deletion marker prevents stale replicas from automatically restoring that memory. This does not remove the same information from separately retained conversations, originals, tasks, exported files or older recovery copies; handle those separately where needed.

Optional private iCloud storage

If you enable iCloud sync or backup, Northstar sends workspace records and attachment contents to your private CloudKit database in your Apple account. Blackhaven does not receive a copy of this private database. Apple provides the storage and transport under its own privacy and iCloud terms. Your Apple sign-in identity and device iCloud account can differ; Northstar checks the linked cloud account before changing cloud records.

Sign in with Apple

Northstar requests an Apple account identifier to protect access to the local signed-in workspace. It does not request your name or email address. The account link is stored in the device Keychain. Northstar does not operate an account-token server or store Apple access or refresh tokens.

No advertising or developer analytics

The native app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or a developer-operated analytics service. Apple may provide crash diagnostics according to your device and TestFlight sharing settings. Do not include sensitive content in feedback unless it is needed to investigate your request.

Retention and recovery

Workspace data remains until you remove it. Synced deletions propagate to updated connected devices. Versioned iCloud backups retain ten recent versions and one version for each of up to thirty distinct UTC dates, separately per device. Pruning occurs during subsequent backups, not at a guaranteed expiration time. Recovery copies can contain earlier conversations and memories; deliberately restoring an older backup can restore older information. Local automatic-sync safety copies are bounded by count and storage size in current release candidates. User-created exports remain wherever you save them, contain readable personal information, and should be protected and shared intentionally. Pausing sync or backup does not delete existing cloud copies.

Account deletion and your choices

Use Settings → Delete account on iOS or Delete account and data in the Mac account dashboard to remove the signed-in workspace on that device, including conversations, memories, attachments, local drafts and recovery copies, and its Northstar iCloud workspace and backups. Deletion requires access to the original iCloud account and can be retried if interrupted. Guest workspaces, exported files and copies on other devices remain; remove those separately if desired. Updated clients are prevented from uploading to a deleted cloud workspace. A minimal cloud deletion fence is retained to enforce that rule.

Deleting Northstar data does not delete your Apple Account. Signing out locks access through Northstar and does not erase saved files or cloud recovery copies. Because Northstar does not retain Apple access tokens, refresh tokens or authorization codes for later revocation, it does not automatically revoke Apple authorization when account data is deleted. It responds to Apple’s credential-revoked notification by closing authenticated access. Follow Apple’s instructions to revoke Sign in with Apple authorization separately. Revoking shared Blackhaven authorization can affect other apps using that authorization.

External services and support

Links you choose to open, such as maps or ChatGPT, are governed by the destination service’s privacy practices. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send and use it to answer and investigate your request. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable recordkeeping obligations. This website’s hosting provider may process ordinary request logs; that is separate from the native app’s private workspace.

Contact and updates

Contact Blackhaven at ceo@atlasmasa.com for privacy questions or requests. Material changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
