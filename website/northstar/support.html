Northstar support

Northstar by Blackhaven · iPhone, iPad and Mac

Email ceo@atlasmasa.com for help. Include your app version/build, device model, OS version, the steps that failed, and any visible error. Avoid sending financial documents or personal conversations unless needed and requested.

Continue between devices

Update both devices and use the same iCloud account. Enable automatic workspace sync once on each device: iPhone Settings → Data protection; Mac Import & transfer → Workspace sync. Wait for “Synced” after setup. Saved offline changes retry when connectivity and app execution resume. iOS may suspend an unfinished background transfer.

Backups and recovery

Sync and backup are separate: sync carries current changes, including deletions; versioned backups provide recovery. Wait for a verified backup before erasing or replacing a device. Review the chosen recovery copy before restoring. Restoring pauses automatic sync so you can review recovered data before reconnecting it.

If sync needs attention

Keep your local data. Check the linked iCloud account, available iCloud storage and connection. Review competing changes in the app. Current hardened release candidates bound automatic sync to 64 MB encoded workspace and 40 MB of attachment contents; larger workspaces remain local and can use complete exports/backups. Do not delete the app to troubleshoot before exporting a verified backup.

Delete account data

Use the in-app Delete account flow while connected to the original iCloud account. Export anything you want to keep first. Guest data, exported backups and copies on other devices require separate removal. To revoke Apple authorization afterwards, follow Apple’s instructions.

Report a crash

For TestFlight, use Send Beta Feedback and include the build number and approximate time. You can also email support. A screenshot of an error is useful; redact private information.
